# Translation and Chāyā

The text pane toolbar switches between three readings of the edition. **Transliteration** is the
linked, syllable-by-syllable text; the other two are continuous prose.

## Translation

The edition's running translation, as a single body of text.

## Chaya

The chāyā — the Sanskrit or Middle Indic rendering the editor supplies alongside the Gāndhārī.

## What changes when you switch

Both are prose, not annotations, so the linking that drives the rest of the Viewer does not apply
here:

- Nothing in the text is clickable, and nothing highlights on the image.
- The current selection is cleared.
- The Details pane collapses, and stays collapsed until you return to Transliteration.
- The image pane is untouched — it keeps its canvas, zoom, and position.

A mode with no content for this edition simply shows an empty pane.

::: tip Aligned instead of continuous
For translation or chāyā **aligned to the text unit by unit** rather than run together, stay in
Transliteration and turn on **Structure**, then **Translation** or **Chaya**, in the transliteration
settings. See [Reading the Transliteration](/viewer/transliteration#structure).
:::

Both also appear as their own sections in [the edition report](/viewer/edition-report), which is the
easiest way to read or print the translation end to end.
