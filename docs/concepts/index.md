# Core Concepts

The Viewer and the Editor are two views onto the same body of data. This section explains the ideas
they share. Read it once; both guides assume it.

## The short version

A **text** is a work — a sūtra, a document, a letter. An **edition** is one scholar's reading of one
witness to that text, tied to a set of manuscript **images**. Within an edition, the reading is
recorded as **tokens** (roughly, words) positioned on the image, each made of **aksaras** (the units
of the Kharoṣṭhī script). A token may carry a **lemma**, which may in turn point at a **canonical
lemma** in Sanskrit or Pali.

The Editor is where those records are created. The Viewer presents them.
