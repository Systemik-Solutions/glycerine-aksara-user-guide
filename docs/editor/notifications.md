# Notifications

The **bell** in the header carries anything the Editor needs to tell you about — in practice, the
outcome of a [lemma review request](/editor/lemmas#private-canonical-and-the-road-between). A badge
shows the unread count.

## Reading them

Opening the bell lists your notices, unread ones marked. Opening the panel does **not** mark
everything read: a rejection carries the reviewer's note, and clearing it on sight would lose the
one place that feedback appears. Click a notice to mark it read, or **Mark all read** to clear the
badge.

Read notices stay in the list, so an approval from last month is still there after the badge has
gone.

**Nothing yet.** means what it says.

## When the count refreshes

Unread counts are fetched when you sign in and again whenever you return to the tab, so a session
left open all day stays current without a reload.

For administrators, the same refresh updates the red dot on the **wrench** — the count of lemma
requests waiting in the [review queue](/editor/canonical-lemmas).
